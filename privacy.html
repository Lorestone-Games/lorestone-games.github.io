Website privacy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This notice covers the Lorestone Games website, lorestonegames.com. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy; for JournalScape, see the JournalScape privacy policy.

What this website collects

Nothing on purpose. This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it loads nothing from other companies.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you, and we don't share them.
